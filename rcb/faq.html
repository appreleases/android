What do I need to use the app?

You must provide the Refresh Token from your new account.

How do I get the Refresh Token?

Option 1: Get the Refresh Token directly from the app (recommended)

  1. Open RCBot and tap on Click to setup
  2. Tap the RollerCoin icon in the top-right corner to open the embedded browser
  3. Log in to your RollerCoin account
  4. Tap COPY REFRESH TOKEN
  5. Close the embedded browser
  6. Paste the token into the app and tap Save and Verify

Option 2: Get the Refresh Token from a PC (manual method)

  1. Log in to RollerCoin using your browser
  2. Press F12 to open Developer Tools
  3. Go to the Application tab
  4. Open Storage / Local Storage / https://rollercoin.com
  5. Copy the refreshToken
  6. Paste it into the app and tap Save and Verify

How do I start from zero?
  1. Play normally until you get RST from daily tasks
  2. Open Common Parts boxes and sell the obtained parts

🚀 With 0.15+ RLT you can start using the bot with Uncommon Parts.

How do I activate the bot?
  1. 📧 Send an email to the administrator
  2. 🧪 The administrator activates your Trial
  3. ✅ Use the app during the trial period
What happens when the trial expires?
